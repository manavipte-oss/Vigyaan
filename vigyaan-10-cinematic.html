Ten Frontiers

Find your sphere of impact.

Every edition of Vigyaan is orchestrated by 10 specialized departments. Choose up to three teams that match your passions, skills, or future aspirations.

Technical

Build the official portal, manage real-time registrations, and host premier hackathons and coding tournaments.

Creatives

Create visual branding, event identities, large-scale stage backdrops, posters, and atmospheric physical installations.

Production

Own the backstage flow, main auditorium lighting, acoustics, audio consoles, and technical live production.

Media

Cinematography, dynamic aftermovies, live photography, and editorial coverage capturing every milestone.

Social Media

Direct the digital narrative, manage reels, viral countdowns, and build the online hype across campus.

PR & Outreach

Liaise with premier corporate sponsors, industry keynote speakers, and colleges across Mumbai.

Admin

The operational core. Timetables, permission letters, college administration coordination, and cross-team alignment.

Hospitality

Host dignitaries, keynote scientists, judges, and visiting college contingents with meticulous care.

Finance

Budget allocation, sponsor receipt management, contingency reserves, and strict financial auditing.

Fashion Walk

Curate, script, and direct the festival's flagship personality, intellect, and science communication showcase.

Edition 4.0 — Inter-Collegiate Expansion

Vigyaan grows beyond college borders, hosting over 40 colleges across Maharashtra for robotics and project competitions.
